TOWER OF TUMBLES - music
========================
Bouncy platformer music, the sort an obby wants (Archer asked for "more roblox style" after the
first, calmer set). Real music by real composers, downloaded from OpenGameArt.org on 2026-09-22
with Archer's OK and used unchanged. Every track here is CC0 (public domain): no attribution is
required, but the game shows the title and composer when each one starts anyway.
